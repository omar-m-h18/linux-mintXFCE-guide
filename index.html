Where Are My Files? (Thunar File Manager)

The biggest difference Windows switchers notice is the absence of a C:\ drive. In Linux Mint, your computer uses a unified filesystem tree. All your personal files reside safely inside your Home folder.

🏠 Your Home Folder

Located at /home/yourname/. It contains your familiar Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Pictures, and Videos folders.

💾 USB Flash Drives

Plug in a flash drive or external hard drive and it appears instantly in the Thunar sidebar under Devices or /media/.

🛡️ Safety By Separation

System files belong to the operating system; your files belong to you. An app cannot accidentally overwrite your files without explicit permission.

Demystifying the Terminal (A Fear-Free Sandbox)

Myth: "You have to be a computer hacker to use Linux."
Truth: You never have to touch the terminal for daily work. However, when someone on a friendly Mint forum gives you a quick solution, understanding what those words mean turns fear into a superpower!

💬 It's Just Text Commands

A terminal is simply a conversation where you type a simple verb (like ls to list files) and it answers directly with plain text.

🛡️ The "sudo" Safety Key

sudo is just the Linux equivalent of right-clicking and choosing "Run as Administrator" in Windows. It asks for your password to protect you.

📋 Copy & Paste Magic

Inside the Linux terminal, shortcut to paste is Ctrl + Shift + V. It makes troubleshooting forum fixes instantaneous.
